01 THE HAWTHORNE IDEA

We take the work
of growing up
seriously.

Hawthorne began with a simple belief: children do their best learning when they feel safe enough to be curious and supported enough to take a risk.

Our teachers know their students well, our community makes room for many perspectives, and our days balance deep focus with the freedom to explore. We serve students from kindergarten through grade 8.
