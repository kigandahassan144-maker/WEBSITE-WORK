02 THE PRACTICAL DETAILS

A few things
families ask.

What grades does Hawthorne serve?

We welcome students from kindergarten through grade 8 across three age-appropriate learning stages.

Can we visit before applying?

Absolutely. Contact us to arrange a tour or an upcoming open house.

Is financial assistance available?

Need-based tuition assistance is available. Our admissions team can explain the process privately.

When should we get in touch?

Any time. Starting early gives your family more chances to visit and choose a timeline that works.
